# CTF Rules

Welcome to **CYBERDUNE CTF**. Learn, compete fairly, and respect the people and systems around you. By participating, you agree to follow these rules.

## 01 — Stay within scope

Attack only the challenge targets explicitly provided by the organizers. The platform itself, login system, scoreboard, host servers, other players' devices, and external services are out of scope unless a challenge explicitly says otherwise. Access to one challenge does not authorize access to other containers or the host.

## 02 — Play fairly

Use one account per player. Do not impersonate others, create extra accounts to bypass limits, manipulate scores, or exploit the submission system. In team events, collaborate only with your registered teammates and follow the announced team-size limit. In individual events, solve independently.

## 03 — Keep flags and solutions private

During an active competition, do not exchange flags, solutions, challenge files, private hints, or working exploits with other teams or individual competitors. Do not publish write-ups, stream solutions, or post spoilers until the organizers allow it. Outside competitions, respect challenge-specific spoiler restrictions and use spoiler warnings when discussing practice challenges.

## 04 — Protect availability

No denial-of-service attacks, traffic flooding, destructive actions, or attempts to exhaust shared resources. Do not brute-force accounts, flags, or platform endpoints. Use scanners and automation only against authorized challenge targets, at a reasonable rate, and follow any challenge-specific restrictions. Stop if your activity disrupts the service or staff ask you to stop.

## 05 — Respect other players' work

Do not attack other participants, steal their credentials or flags, interrupt their instances, or delete or modify shared challenge data to block progress. Do not access another team's environment unless the event explicitly uses an attack-defense format and authorizes it.

## 06 — Submit flags correctly

Submit flags through the challenge's submission form, exactly as found. The usual format is `CYBERDUNE{...}` unless the challenge specifies another format. Do not send flags to other players or post them in public support messages. Scores, hint costs, and attempt limits are determined by the platform and event settings.

## 07 — Keep the community respectful

No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or abusive team names and profile content. Treat participants and organizers respectfully. Social engineering of players or staff is forbidden unless it is explicitly part of an authorized challenge.

## 08 — Report problems responsibly

If you discover an unintended vulnerability in the platform or access data outside a challenge, stop testing and report it privately to the organizers at [cyberdunecontact@gmail.com](mailto:cyberdunecontact@gmail.com). Include the challenge name, a short description, and minimal evidence. Do not extract additional data, publish the issue, or use it to gain points. Never include passwords or access tokens in your report.

## 09 — Follow event announcements

Challenge-specific instructions and official event announcements may add restrictions or explain exceptions. Check them before playing. Use only the help and external tools permitted for that event; do not assume outside assistance or AI tools are allowed. If a rule is unclear, ask the organizers before proceeding.

## 10 — Enforcement

Depending on the severity of a violation, organizers may issue a warning, remove submissions or points, disqualify a player or team, or suspend an account. Serious or repeated abuse may lead to an immediate ban. Raise questions about a decision privately with the organizers.

**Play fair. Stay in scope. Enjoy the hunt.**
