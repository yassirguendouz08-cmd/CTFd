Rules of Engagement

Zero-tolerance operational standards enforced by the review committee.

crisis_alert 01. Scope Strictness

Attacking targets outside assigned challenge ranges or target domains is strictly prohibited. Subnet boundary violations trigger immediate squad disqualification.

shield 02. Infrastructure Protection

Denial of service (DoS), deliberate exhaustion of resources, and attacks against the central scoring engine or challenge orchestrator will result in an instant ban.

share_off 03. No Flag Sharing

Flags are strictly unique tokens. Exchanging writeups, clues, or flags between separate squads during live competition windows is strictly forbidden.
