ETHICAL BOUNDARIES & INTEGRITY

Rules of Engagement

ENFORCED BY CTF MARSHALS
01. Scope Strictness

Designated challenge targets strictly in scope. Scoring infrastructure, judging instances, hosting clusters, and fellow participants are strictly off-limits.

02. Zero Denial of Service

Denial of service (DoS/DDoS) against platform services, challenge network daemons, or internet uplinks results in immediate disqualification.

03. No Flag Sharing

Sharing flags, solutions, hints, or exploit scripts between squads prior to the official round closure is strictly prohibited.
